## What fab0 does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="You describe the line" icon="pencil">
		Say what starts the work, who or what does each step, and who decides. Claude writes it down as a
		pipeline, and fab0 checks it before anything runs.
	</Card>
	<Card title="fab0 runs it" icon="rocket">
		Each step goes to the worker you named: an agent behind a URL, a Claude Code routine, or a person's
		queue. Retries, deadlines and loops are part of the pipeline, not of a prompt.
	</Card>
	<Card title="You always know where work stands" icon="list-format">
		Ask "what's going on?" at any time. fab0 says what is running, what waits on whom, and what failed,
		and keeps the full record of each run.
	</Card>
	<Card title="It decides nothing for you" icon="approve-check">
		fab0 never judges, approves or merges your work. Your people and agents make every decision, and the
		record names who made it.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
